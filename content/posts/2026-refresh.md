Apparently this blog only ever writes about constructing itself.

I was struggling with giving this site a refresh. Over the last few months I was off and on it again for a few days at a time. Turns out typography is important!

In hindsight, it explains why everything felt pretty amateurish. Focusing only on colour and layout was literally style with no substance, or at least structure.

Anyways, after a lot of experimentation to figure out what I liked (LLM-generated config playgrounds are great for this), I can confidently say it's *less* amateurish. In few words I've settled on a vertical grid system, metadata in left marginalia, and a serif reading font. Now it feels like I have a small jewelry box and no jewelry.

Naturally I could keep tweaking details forever but I'm going to practice restraint. My eyes are now open to world of typography and it's starting to click how similar it can be to good software. My frontier of interests keeps growing via metaphor.

Happy to be free of those polaroids. Not sure what I was thinking.
